Privacy Policy

Saku · Erek Studio

Short version: The app saves your progress on your device. Google AdMob serves ads in the free app. Your app store handles optional purchases and any review you submit. The web daily counts visits, solves and store-link clicks using a random browser identifier.

What we collect

Saku has no account or profile. Google handles data for app advertising as described below. Erek Studio records the web daily events described in the Web daily section.

Data stored on your device

Saku saves a few things locally so the app remembers your choices:

  • Your settings (theme, haptics, error-guard, accent palette).
  • Your puzzle progress and which pictures you have solved.
  • Whether you have purchased the one-time Studio Unlock.

The app stores this information locally. Deleting the app removes its local data.

Advertising

A short ad may appear after a solved puzzle in the free app. You can also choose to watch an ad for a single hint or to open a past daily puzzle or a Pro album picture. Ads are served by Google AdMob. To do that, Google processes your advertising identifier, an approximate region derived from your IP address, device and app information, and your interaction with the ad, under Google's privacy policy. We only ever see aggregate revenue reporting — never anything about an individual person.

In the EEA, Switzerland, and the UK, a Google consent form (UMP) asks for your choice before the first ad loads. You can change it at any time from Settings → Privacy options in the app. On iOS the system additionally asks whether the app may track you across apps and websites; if you decline, you still see ads, but they are not personalized.

The one-time Studio Unlock removes ads. When the unlock is already active, Saku skips the ad SDK and its consent and tracking prompts at launch.

Network & third parties

The mobile app runs its puzzles on your device. Its ad SDK connects to Google during free play. The mobile app bundles no analytics or crash-reporting SDKs.

Web daily

When you play Saku’s web daily, Saku stores a randomly generated, anonymous identifier in your browser’s local storage. We use it to count distinct browsers that open a puzzle, solve it or click a store link. Each event includes the date, the puzzle number, the event type and that random identifier. You can remove the identifier by clearing Saku’s site data in your browser.

The web daily uses no cookies and contains no ads. Saku uses IP addresses briefly in server memory to limit repeated requests. Saku does not store IP addresses in analytics or request logs. The page may load fonts from Google Fonts to display text in your language.

Sharing your daily result

Saku prepares a share message on your device. It includes the daily puzzle number, grid size, hint count and a link to the puzzle. The share feature sends nothing unless you choose to share. The app or service you choose handles the message under its own privacy policy.

Purchases & reviews

The Apple App Store or Google Play processes the optional Studio Unlock purchase and any rating or review you submit, under the store’s own privacy policies.

Children

Saku is not directed at children. Ads in the free app can link to external sites. Studio Unlock removes advertising in the app.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, we will update this page and its effective date.
